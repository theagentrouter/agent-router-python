# Acme data export

This is fictional example product documentation.

## Export before cancellation

An account administrator can export records from Settings → Data → Export.

Choose CSV for records or JSON for a full structured export. The export is a ZIP download and includes attachments.

Exporting records does not cancel a subscription. Complete the export before requesting account deletion.

## Access after cancellation

Canceling renewal keeps the account accessible until the end of the current paid period unless billing approves an early termination.

The refund policy does not specify when access ends after a refund. Ask billing support to confirm access timing before promising a date.

## Deletion

Account deletion removes access immediately and is separate from cancellation. Deleted account data is removed from active storage within 30 days.
