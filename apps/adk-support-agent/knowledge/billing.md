# Acme billing and refunds

This is fictional example policy for the sample agent.

## Annual subscriptions

A first annual subscription payment is eligible for a full refund within 14 calendar days of payment if the account has not used paid features.

Annual renewals and accounts that have used paid features require a billing specialist to review the request. Agents must not promise a refund in these cases.

Canceling a subscription stops its next renewal. Cancellation does not create a refund automatically.

## Refund request

Ask for the invoice ID and the account email. Never ask for a full card number, password, or API key.

The billing specialist checks payment date and paid-feature usage before approving the refund.

Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. The time starts when the refund is approved, not when the request is submitted.

## Escalation

Send payment disputes, duplicate charges, and exceptions to billing support. The assistant can prepare a request but cannot approve or execute refunds.
